Tripod Setup

Four camera tripods, one off each corner of the court

Tripod placement

Distance: from the court corner, outward along the diagonal.
Height: floor to camera lens.
Angle: camera direction measured from the back boundary line, facing into the court.
Tilt: camera pointed down from level so the near corner and margin sit in the lower part of the frame.
Lens FOV: horizontal field of view of a 16:9 camera.
